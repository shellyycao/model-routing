## Analysis questions and the metric_keys each must produce
- q1: Overall (aggregation): number of customers (rows) -> customer_count; percentage of customers with churned = 1 -> churn_rate_pct, expressed as a percentage; mean monthly_fee -> avg_monthly_fee.
  metric_keys: customer_count, churn_rate_pct, avg_monthly_fee
- q2: Churn by plan (group comparison): churn rate in percent among customers of each plan -> churn_rate_basic_pct, churn_rate_standard_pct, churn_rate_premium_pct.
  metric_keys: churn_rate_basic_pct, churn_rate_standard_pct, churn_rate_premium_pct
- q3: Churn by signup cohort (trend): churn rate in percent among customers whose signup_date falls in each calendar year -> churn_rate_cohort_2021_pct, churn_rate_cohort_2022_pct, churn_rate_cohort_2023_pct, churn_rate_cohort_2024_pct.
  metric_keys: churn_rate_cohort_2021_pct, churn_rate_cohort_2022_pct, churn_rate_cohort_2023_pct, churn_rate_cohort_2024_pct
- q4: Contract effect (percentage change): churn rate in percent for Monthly contracts -> churn_rate_monthly_pct; for Annual contracts -> churn_rate_annual_pct; relative difference = (churn_rate_monthly_pct - churn_rate_annual_pct) / churn_rate_annual_pct * 100 -> churn_monthly_vs_annual_change_pct.
  metric_keys: churn_rate_monthly_pct, churn_rate_annual_pct, churn_monthly_vs_annual_change_pct
- q5: At-risk segment (conditional filtering): customers with support_tickets >= 5 AND a non-missing satisfaction_score <= 2: their number -> at_risk_customer_count; their churn rate in percent -> at_risk_churn_rate_pct.
  metric_keys: at_risk_customer_count, at_risk_churn_rate_pct
- q6: Tenure and satisfaction summary (statistical summary): mean, median and sample standard deviation (denominator n - 1) of tenure_months over all customers -> tenure_mean, tenure_median, tenure_std; mean satisfaction_score over customers with a non-missing score -> satisfaction_mean; number of customers with a missing satisfaction_score -> satisfaction_missing_count.
  metric_keys: tenure_mean, tenure_median, tenure_std, satisfaction_mean, satisfaction_missing_count
- q7: Lifetime value by plan (derived step): mean lifetime_value for each plan -> ltv_basic, ltv_standard, ltv_premium; (ltv_premium - ltv_basic) / ltv_basic * 100 -> premium_vs_basic_ltv_change_pct.
  metric_keys: ltv_basic, ltv_standard, ltv_premium, premium_vs_basic_ltv_change_pct

All required metric_keys: customer_count, churn_rate_pct, avg_monthly_fee, churn_rate_basic_pct, churn_rate_standard_pct, churn_rate_premium_pct, churn_rate_cohort_2021_pct, churn_rate_cohort_2022_pct, churn_rate_cohort_2023_pct, churn_rate_cohort_2024_pct, churn_rate_monthly_pct, churn_rate_annual_pct, churn_monthly_vs_annual_change_pct, at_risk_customer_count, at_risk_churn_rate_pct, tenure_mean, tenure_median, tenure_std, satisfaction_mean, satisfaction_missing_count, ltv_basic, ltv_standard, ltv_premium, premium_vs_basic_ltv_change_pct
