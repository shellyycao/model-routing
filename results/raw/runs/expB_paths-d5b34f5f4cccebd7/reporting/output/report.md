## Limitations
- The analysis is limited to the cleaned order export; returns, cancellations, or post‑sale adjustments are not reflected.
- Discount percentages are taken as recorded; any promotional nuances (e.g., bundled offers) are not captured.
- Only two years of data are available, which may not represent longer‑term trends or seasonality beyond 2024.

## Conclusion
Retail sales are growing, driven by strong performance in the East region and a notable contribution from high‑discount Electronics orders. While average order value is healthy, revenue distribution is highly skewed, with the top customer accounting for over 6% of sales. Stakeholders should consider targeted regional strategies, monitor discount impacts, and explore ways to broaden the customer base to reduce concentration risk.
