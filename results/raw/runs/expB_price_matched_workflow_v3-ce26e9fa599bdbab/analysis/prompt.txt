## Analysis questions and the metric_keys each must produce
- q1: Overall performance (aggregation): total revenue (sum of revenue) -> total_revenue; number of orders (rows in the cleaned data) -> order_count; average order value = total_revenue / order_count -> average_order_value.
  metric_keys: total_revenue, order_count, average_order_value
- q2: Regional comparison (group comparison): total revenue per region -> revenue_north, revenue_south, revenue_east, revenue_west.
  metric_keys: revenue_north, revenue_south, revenue_east, revenue_west
- q3: Quarterly trend in 2024 (trend): total revenue of orders dated in each calendar quarter of 2024 (Q1 = Jan-Mar, Q2 = Apr-Jun, Q3 = Jul-Sep, Q4 = Oct-Dec) -> revenue_2024_q1, revenue_2024_q2, revenue_2024_q3, revenue_2024_q4.
  metric_keys: revenue_2024_q1, revenue_2024_q2, revenue_2024_q3, revenue_2024_q4
- q4: Year-over-year change (percentage change): total revenue of orders dated in 2023 -> revenue_2023; in 2024 -> revenue_2024; percentage change = (revenue_2024 - revenue_2023) / revenue_2023 * 100 -> revenue_yoy_change_pct, expressed as a percentage.
  metric_keys: revenue_2023, revenue_2024, revenue_yoy_change_pct
- q5: High-discount electronics (conditional filtering): among orders with product_category 'Electronics' and discount_pct >= 20: number of orders -> electronics_high_discount_orders; their total revenue -> electronics_high_discount_revenue.
  metric_keys: electronics_high_discount_orders, electronics_high_discount_revenue
- q6: Order revenue distribution (statistical summary) over all orders: mean -> order_revenue_mean; median -> order_revenue_median; sample standard deviation (denominator n - 1) -> order_revenue_std; minimum -> order_revenue_min; maximum -> order_revenue_max.
  metric_keys: order_revenue_mean, order_revenue_median, order_revenue_std, order_revenue_min, order_revenue_max
- q7: Customer concentration (derived step): number of distinct customer_id values -> unique_customer_count; revenue of the highest-revenue customer -> top_customer_revenue; top_customer_revenue / total revenue * 100 -> top_customer_revenue_share_pct.
  metric_keys: unique_customer_count, top_customer_revenue, top_customer_revenue_share_pct

All required metric_keys: total_revenue, order_count, average_order_value, revenue_north, revenue_south, revenue_east, revenue_west, revenue_2024_q1, revenue_2024_q2, revenue_2024_q3, revenue_2024_q4, revenue_2023, revenue_2024, revenue_yoy_change_pct, electronics_high_discount_orders, electronics_high_discount_revenue, order_revenue_mean, order_revenue_median, order_revenue_std, order_revenue_min, order_revenue_max, unique_customer_count, top_customer_revenue, top_customer_revenue_share_pct
